Human Resources Business Partner
----------------------------------------
We are seeking an experienced Human Resources Business Partner to serve as a strategic advisor to leadership teams while managing the full employee lifecycle. This role requires a dynamic professional who can balance strategic HR initiatives with hands-on operational support, fostering a positive workplace culture and driving organizational effectiveness. You will work closely with department heads to align HR strategies with business objectives, manage talent acquisition and retention programs, and ensure compliance with employment laws and regulations. The ideal candidate will have exceptional interpersonal skills, strong business acumen, and the ability to influence at all levels of the organization.
----------------------------------------
* Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, or related field; Master's degree or MBA preferred
* Minimum 5-7 years of progressive HR experience with at least 3 years in a business partner or strategic HR role
* Professional HR certification such as SHRM-CP, SHRM-SCP, PHR, or SPHR strongly preferred
* Deep knowledge of employment law, HR best practices, and current trends in talent management and organizational development
* Proven experience managing full-cycle recruitment processes and building effective talent acquisition strategies
* Strong business acumen with ability to understand financial statements, business models, and organizational dynamics
* Exceptional interpersonal and communication skills with ability to build trust and influence stakeholders at all organizational levels
* Demonstrated experience with HRIS systems and HR analytics tools; proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite required
* Strong problem-solving and critical thinking abilities with a solutions-oriented mindset
* Ability to handle sensitive and confidential information with discretion and maintain high ethical standards
* Experience leading change management initiatives and supporting organizations through periods of transformation
* Project management skills with ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines simultaneously
----------------------------------------
* Partner with business leaders to understand organizational goals and develop HR strategies that support achievement of business objectives
* Lead talent acquisition efforts including workforce planning, recruitment strategy development, candidate screening, interviewing, and onboarding processes
* Design and implement comprehensive employee development programs including training initiatives, succession planning, and career progression frameworks
* Manage employee relations issues by conducting thorough investigations, providing coaching to managers, mediating conflicts, and recommending appropriate solutions
* Develop and maintain compensation and benefits programs that are competitive, equitable, and aligned with organizational budget and market standards
* Drive performance management processes including goal setting, mid-year reviews, annual evaluations, and performance improvement plans
* Ensure compliance with federal, state, and local employment laws and regulations including FMLA, ADA, FLSA, and EEO requirements
* Analyze HR metrics and workforce data to identify trends, develop insights, and make data-driven recommendations to leadership
* Lead organizational change management initiatives including restructuring, mergers, acquisitions, and cultural transformation projects
* Develop and implement employee engagement strategies including surveys, feedback mechanisms, recognition programs, and retention initiatives
